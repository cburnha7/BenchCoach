## Description (4000)
Bench Coach is the sideline companion for youth soccer coaches. Set your
lineup, keep playing time fair, make subs in a tap, and diagram plays — without
juggling a clipboard and a stopwatch.

EVERY PLAYER, FAIR MINUTES
• Automatic playing-time tracking while the clock runs
• See at a glance who needs a run — the bench sorts by least time played
• Queue subs and run them all at once at the next stoppage

LINEUPS & FORMATIONS
• 7v7, 9v9, and 11v11 with a full set of formations
• Drag players anywhere; one tap resets everyone to their spots
• Give each player a jersey number or a fun emoji badge

TACTICS BOARD
• Draw passes and movement runs that follow your finger
• Show who has the ball with a clear possession marker
• Add an opponent shape to walk through attacking and defensive plays

MATCH DAY
• Simple scoreboard for both teams
• Log goals and assists, hand out yellow and red cards
• Season goal/assist stats for your squad

FAST ROSTER SETUP
• Type players in, or scan a printed roster with your camera

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything stays on your device
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no servers

Built by a coach, for coaches. One-time purchase — no subscription.
