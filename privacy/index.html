Bench Coach — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Bench Coach does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

What we store

Everything you enter — team names, player names, jersey numbers, playing time, team colors, and team photos — is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party. We operate no servers and keep no copy of your data.

Team photos and roster scanning

If you add a team photo or use the roster photo feature, the image is handled entirely on your device and is not uploaded anywhere.

No accounts, no tracking

The app has no login, no analytics, and no advertising. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Deleting a team removes its data from your device. Deleting the app removes all of its data.

Children's privacy

Because all information stays on your device and is never collected or transmitted, no personal information about any person — including minors — is gathered by us.
